Maps exist for many reasons. They exist as tools for navigation and understanding. They illustrate storis within a geographic context. Or they can simple exist as beatiful things we like to look at.We both make maps of places we know and places that we want to know more about. 

This map is a product of homesickness and a desire to render something familiar. 

When I started living in Vermont I was constantly in awe of how green it was (when it wasn't snowing, of course). To put this into perspective. The street that I grew up had just the one tree. Now, looking out my window, I'd have to look straight up into the sky to not see green foliage. 

Living in a place with such bountiful greenery I started thinking alot about how special urban forests can be and how unalike they are to they're un-urban counterparts.
