My name is Ola Zalecki, and welcome to my portfolio site.

I graduated from Middlebury College in 2024, where I earned my Bachelor of Arts in Geography with a minor in Spanish. Shortly after I rejoined the Geography Department as the Assistant in Science Instruction, where I now support introductory methods courses in GIS and cartography.

Long before I took my first geography class, I had gravitated toward learning about people, societies, and their relationships with the physical world. As an undergraduate student, I learned the language and tools that allow me to explore and question our interactions with space and place. My research interests are heavily influenced by my upbringing in Chicago as a Polish-American, as well as my recent years living in rural Vermont. This means I’m passionate about public spaces, immigrant place-making, neighborhoods and, of course, hot dog stands.

I am also deeply passionate about map design! The practice of representing spatial data is a central aspect of my work as a geographer, as I aim to create maps that are accessible, thought-provoking, and engaging.

When I’m not hunched over my computer screen, you’re most likely to find me cooking, gardening, or hanging out near a body of water.
